# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Students and office workers who keep the timer open in a browser tab through long work sessions, plus recruiters and clients who see it as a portfolio piece.

## Product Purpose
A Pomodoro timer: 25 min focus, 5 min short break, 15 min long break after every 4 focus sessions. Success is a timer that is readable at a glance and calm to leave open all day.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- React 19 + TypeScript + Vite, no UI library, no backend.
- Start/pause, reset, phase progress, cycle count, live tab title, chime on phase end with mute.
- UI copy is English. The chime is synthesized with Web Audio (no audio files).

## Brand Commitments
Keeps the original mint green (`#41e1ba`) and tomato red (`#EF5D50`) as the break and focus colors.

## Product Principles
- Time remaining is always the loudest thing on screen.
- Phase is told by color and label together, never color alone.
- Nothing moves except what is counting down.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard operable, visible focus, WCAG AA contrast, respects reduced motion.
